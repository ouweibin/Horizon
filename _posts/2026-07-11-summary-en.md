QuadRF, an open-source RF sensor developed by Scale RF, uses a 4x4 MIMO software-defined radio and a Raspberry Pi 5 to detect drones and visualize WiFi signals passing through walls in real time. This device democratizes RF sensing, enabling hobbyists and researchers to monitor wireless signals for security, drone detection, and spectrum analysis at a fraction of the cost of professional equipment. QuadRF operates as a real-time RF camera rendering radio sources as a 30fps overlay, with a fully open-source software stack under GPLv2/GPLv3, though the RF core and factory DSP bitstreams remain proprietary.

MIMO (multiple-input multiple-output) uses multiple antennas to improve signal detection and direction finding. QuadRF combines these to create an 'RF camera' that can locate sources of RF radiation.

The European Commission preliminarily found that Meta's Facebook and Instagram violate the EU Digital Services Act through addictive design features, including infinite scroll, autoplay, and personalized recommendations. Meta could face a fine of up to $12 billion (6% of global annual revenue) if the charges are confirmed. This action could set a precedent for regulating addictive platform designs globally, forcing Meta and other tech giants to redesign their services to prioritize user well-being. It also highlights the EU's growing enforcement power under the Digital Services Act. EU regulators criticized Meta's existing time-limiting tools as ineffective and demanded that the apps default to disabling addictive features, implement effective screen-break reminders, and weaken engagement-optimized recommendation algorithms. The fine would be the largest ever under the DSA.

**Background**: The Digital Services Act (DSA) is a comprehensive EU regulation that came into force in 2022, imposing strict accountability on large online platforms for content moderation and systemic risks. Addictive design patterns—such as infinite scrolling and algorithmic recommendations—are considered dark patterns that manipulate user behavior and harm mental health, practices that the DSA seeks to curb.
